AKYVAN — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AKYVAN may process information when you use the bot.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, AKYVAN may process information such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, display names, roles, permissions, channel information, moderation actions, server configuration, security settings, and audit log information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide moderation and security features, detect or prevent abuse, perform server management actions, maintain bot configuration, improve reliability and security, and respond to support requests.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services only when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or provide AKYVAN's features.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide AKYVAN's services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

5. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of AKYVAN by contacting the AKYVAN developer through the support contact provided with the bot.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by AKYVAN. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the AKYVAN developer through the support contact provided with the bot.
